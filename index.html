01 / PROFILE

About me

I’m a Cyber Security Analyst specialising in Security Operations Center (SOC) work — monitoring SIEM telemetry, investigating alerts, hunting threats, and reducing mean-time-to-respond through disciplined triage, detection tuning, and root-cause analysis.

My day-to-day spans CrowdStrike Falcon, Splunk ES, Elastic SIEM, Microsoft Sentinel and Qualys VMDR — building and refining detection content, correlating events across endpoints, and mapping adversary behaviour to MITRE ATT&CK. I work across enterprise and OT/ICS environments (ISA/IEC 62443, Siemens S7 PLCs, Modbus, DNP3) within NIST and ISO/IEC 27001 frameworks.

Beyond the console, I build security tooling and labs. My postgraduate research produced a penetration-testing framework for UAV networks (PX4 / Pixhawk 6C, MAVLink), and my SentinelForge platform engineers the full detection-to-incident pipeline — Python, FastAPI, Sysmon, Wazuh, and a versioned detection catalogue with analyst workflow built in.

🎓 MSc Capstone

Penetration Testing Framework for UAV Networks

Designed and executed a reproducible UAV penetration-testing framework on a PX4 Holybro X500 V2 / Pixhawk 6C platform. Evaluated Wi-Fi deauthentication, GPS spoofing, MAVLink C2 hijacking, DoS flooding, and battery/resource exhaustion with unified metrics — disruption latency, recovery time, path deviation, CPU/MCU load. Delivered cross-attack benchmarks, CIA impact mapping, and hardening priorities for UAV operators.

SentinelForge — Endpoint Telemetry & Alerting
